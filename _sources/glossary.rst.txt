========
Glossary
========

.. rubric:: Italian, French, and English Operational and Structural Terms in Compass-Only Geometry

.. glossary::

   La Geometria del Compasso
      Lorenzo Mascheroni's celebrated 1797 treatise establishing that all problems of Euclidean plane geometry that can be solved by compass and straightedge can be solved by compass alone, dispensing entirely with the ruler.

   Géométrographie Mascheronienne
      Evaluation of constructions restricted exclusively to the compass (:math:`l_1 = l_2 = 0`; symbol :math:`m_1 C_1 + m_2 C_2 + m_3 C_3`) following Lorenzo Mascheroni's *La Geometria del Compasso* (1797), where straight lines exist implicitly as ordered pairs of points.

   Retta data per due punti (Virtual Line)
      In compass-only geometry, straight lines cannot be continuously traced; instead, every straight line is represented and uniquely determined by two distinct points :math:`(A, B)`. Intersecting two virtual lines or a virtual line with a circle means finding their intersection points using compass arcs alone.

   Opération C₁
      Setting one leg of the compass on a previously determined point (op.: :math:`C_1`). Taking up the distance :math:`AB` between two determined points with the two legs of the compass requires placing both points and therefore counts as :math:`2C_1`.

   Opération C₂
      Setting one leg of the compass at an arbitrary (indeterminate) point lying on an already traced circular arc (op.: :math:`C_2`), contributing :math:`1` to both simplicity :math:`S` and exactitude :math:`E`.

   Opération C₃
      Describing a circle (or sufficient arc) with the compass once its center and aperture are established (op.: :math:`C_3`). In any compass-only construction symbol, the coefficient :math:`m_3` of :math:`C_3` equals the exact number of circles drawn.

   Coefficient de simplicité (S)
      Total number of elementary operations executed in a compass-only construction: :math:`S = m_1 + m_2 + m_3` (with :math:`l_1 = l_2 = 0`). Lower :math:`S` signifies greater economy of execution.

   Coefficient d'exactitude (E)
      Sum of all preparatory point-coincidence operations prior to tracing: :math:`E = m_1 + m_2`. Because every placement of a compass point introduces a small observational error, fewer preparatory coincidences yield higher graphical precision.

   Raddoppiamento della distanza (Libro III, § 64)
      Mascheroni's fundamental operation stepping three successive chords equal to radius :math:`AB` around circle :math:`B(BA)`, producing point :math:`E` collinear with :math:`A` and :math:`B` such that :math:`AE = 2\,AB` in :math:`5C_1 + 4C_3` (:math:`S = 9`, 4 circles).

   Bisezione d'una distanza al solo compasso (Libro III, § 66, Sol. V)
      Finding the midpoint :math:`M` of an un-drawn segment :math:`AB` without a straightedge: double :math:`AB` to :math:`E` (§ 64), sweep arc :math:`E(EB)` cutting :math:`B(BA)` at :math:`P, p`, then arcs :math:`P(PB)` and :math:`p(PB)` intersect at the exact midpoint :math:`M` of :math:`AB` in 7 circles (:math:`10C_1 + 7C_3, S = 17, E = 10`).

   Inversione circolare (Functor di Adler)
      Conformal involution mapping point :math:`A` to :math:`A'` such that :math:`OA \cdot OA' = R^2`; executed in :math:`5C_1 + 3C_3` (:math:`S = 8`) by compass alone. Discovered by :person:`August Adler <adler>` (1890) to unify Mascheroni's Book VII by transforming straight lines into circles passing through the inversion center.

   Quarta proporzionale per cerchi concentrici (Libro V, § 93)
      Given three segments :math:`a, b, c`, draw concentric circles :math:`O(a)` and :math:`O(b)`, place chord :math:`BC = c` in :math:`O(a)`, and step equal arcs :math:`BD = CE` onto :math:`O(b)`; chord :math:`DE = bc/a` in just 4 circles (:math:`S = 14`), celebrated by :person:`Gaetano Fazzari <fazzari>` (1901) as simpler than Euclidean straightedge constructions.

   Problema di Napoleone (Libro X, § 143)
      Finding the center of a given circle using only the compass. In § 143 (Fig. 71), Mascheroni marks a semicircle BCDE centered on a boundary point A cutting the given circle at M, constructs the third proportional BQ to EM and EA via arcs E(EM) ∩ A(EM) = L and L(LA) ∩ BCDE = Q, and intersects B(BQ) ∩ A(BQ) at center O (later reformulated via circular inversion by August Adler in 1890).

   Géométrographie Steinerienne
      The exact dual regime to Mascheroni: evaluation of constructions executed by straightedge alone (:math:`m_1 = m_2 = m_3 = 0`; symbol :math:`l_1 R_1 + l_2 R_2`) given a single permanently drawn circle with known center :math:`M`, following :person:`Jakob Steiner <steiner>` (1833).
