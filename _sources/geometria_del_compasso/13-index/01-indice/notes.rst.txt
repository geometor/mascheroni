Archival Table of Contents (Indice) — Critical Notes
====================================================

Witness & Folio Provenance
--------------------------

* **Archival Witness:** :cite:`fazzari-1901` (Palermo 1901 centenary reprint of :cite:`mascheroni-1797`)
* **Folio Span:** ``0177``–``0181`` (printed pp. ``152``–``endpaper-4``)

Folio ``0177`` prints the original *Indice* listing exact page starts for all twelve books (confirming that Book III originally opened on printed p. 31 inside the 4-page Harvard scan lacuna pp. 28–31). Folios ``0178``–``0181`` are the blank back endpapers of the bound volume.
