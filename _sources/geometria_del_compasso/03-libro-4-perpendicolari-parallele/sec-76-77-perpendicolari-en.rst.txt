==========================================================
Book IV §§ 76–77: Perpendicular Lines with Compass Alone
==========================================================

.. rubric:: Lorenzo Mascheroni (*The Geometry of the Compass*, Pavia 1797, §§ 76–77, pp. 42–43)

.. folio:: 0067

76. *Problem.* Two points :math:`A` and :math:`B` being given (fig. 26); to find a point :math:`H` such that the straight line :math:`BH` is perpendicular to :math:`AB` at :math:`B` and equal to a given distance :math:`CD`.

.. picture:: /_static/figures/plates/fig_26.png
   :alt: Mascheroni Fig. 26 (Book IV § 76)

*Solution.* Double :math:`AB` in a straight line so that :math:`BE = AB` (§ 64). Then with centers :math:`A` and :math:`E` and radius :math:`CD`, describe two arcs intersecting in :math:`H`. The point :math:`H` will be the sought point.

*Demonstration.* Since by construction :math:`AH = EH = CD`, and the base :math:`AE` is bisected at :math:`B`, the line :math:`BH` is the altitude of isosceles triangle :math:`\triangle AHE`, and is therefore perpendicular to :math:`AE`, that is to :math:`AB` (Euclid I.8, I.13). Which was to be constructed.

77. *Problem.* Two points :math:`A` and :math:`B` being given; to find a point :math:`D` such that :math:`DA` is perpendicular to :math:`AB` (fig. 27).

.. picture:: /_static/figures/plates/fig_27.png
   :alt: Mascheroni Fig. 27 (Book IV § 77)

*Solution.* With an arbitrary radius greater than half of :math:`AB`, describe two arcs from centers :math:`A` and :math:`B` intersecting in :math:`C`. With center :math:`C` and radius :math:`CA`, describe semicircle :math:`BAD` (§ 64). The point :math:`D` will be the sought point.

*Demonstration.* Since :math:`BD` is a diameter of the circle with center :math:`C`, angle :math:`\angle BAD` is inscribed in a semicircle; hence it is a right angle (Euclid III.31). Therefore :math:`DA` is perpendicular to :math:`AB` at point :math:`A`.
