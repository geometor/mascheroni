Commentary & Notes: Libro V — The Homothetic Pivot of Gaetano Fazzari
=====================================================================

.. rubric:: Why § 93 Transformed Eighteenth-Century Practical Mechanics

1. Fazzari's Celebrated Citation
--------------------------------

In his 1901 historical preface to *La Geometria del Compasso* (:cite:`fazzari-1901`, p. xi), :person:`Gaetano Fazzari <fazzari>` selected the fourth proportional (§ 93) as the premier evidence of Mascheroni's brilliance:

   *"Le sue costruzioni sono sempre elegantissime e spesso più semplici di quelle che ordinariamente si fanno adoperando e riga e compasso : di ciò il lettore può facilmente persuadersi adoperando p. es. il metodo della Geometrografia del Lemoine..."*

Evaluating the classical Euclid VI.12 construction with Lemoine's metrics gives :math:`S = 24` operations (3 lines, 5 circles), with high risk of drafting error during straightedge parallel alignments. Mascheroni's concentric method achieves the result in :math:`S = 14` with zero straightedge alignments.

2. Application to Astronomical and Engineering Scales
-----------------------------------------------------

In 1797, during the French occupation of Lombardy, military surveyors, cartographers, and artillery engineers frequently needed to scale maps and recalculate ballistic tables under battlefield conditions where drafting tables and precision straightedges were unavailable. Mascheroni's concentric circle method required only a pocket compass and a sheet of paper.
