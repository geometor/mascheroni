======================================================
§§ 27–29: Quadrisection of the Circumference — Summary
======================================================

.. folio:: 0048
   :page: 19

Circle quadrisection without straightedge (§ 27, Fig. 9): stepping radius :math:`1` yields :math:`BD = \sqrt{3}`, arcs :math:`B(\sqrt{3}) \cap E(\sqrt{3}) = a` give :math:`Aa = \sqrt{2}`, cutting the circle at exact square vertices :math:`B, F, E, f`.
