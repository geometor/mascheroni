===========================================
§§ 30–35: Division into 8, 12, and 24 Parts
===========================================

.. folio:: 0049
   :page: 20

**§§ 30–32.** Building directly on Fig. 9 (folio 0048), Mascheroni divides the circumference into **8 equal parts** (:math:`45^\circ`) by sweeping unit radius :math:`AB = 1` from the quadrisection auxiliary point :math:`a` (cutting the circle at :math:`G` and :math:`H`, since :math:`AG = 1, Aa = \sqrt{2}, aG = 1 \implies \angle A a G = 45^\circ`), into **12 equal parts** (:math:`30^\circ`) by stepping unit radius from :math:`F` and :math:`f`, and into **24 equal parts** (:math:`15^\circ`) by combining the 8-part and 12-part divisions (:math:`\gcd(8,12)` yields :math:`45^\circ - 30^\circ = 15^\circ`).

**§§ 33–35.** Mascheroni stresses the extraordinary instrumental economy: all 24 divisions require only the two apertures :math:`1` and :math:`\sqrt{2}` plus the single helper point :math:`a`.
