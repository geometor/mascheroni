=============================================================
§§ 45–63: Golden Ratio Proof, 240-Part Circle & Arc Bisection
=============================================================

.. folio:: 0055
   :page: 26

**§§ 45–52 (pp. 26–27, folio 0055–folio 0056).** Mascheroni proves rigorously that point :math:`b` of Fig. 12 divides radius :math:`AB` in **extreme and mean ratio** (:math:`AB : Ab :: Ab : Bb`, § 46), verifies Euclid Book XIII Prop. 10 (:math:`(\text{pentagon})^2 = (\text{hexagon})^2 + (\text{decagon})^2`, § 50), and extends the exact divisions to 20, 60, and 120 parts.

**§§ 53–63 (pp. 28–30, Witness Scan Lacuna).** In the closing sections of Book II—spanning printed pp. 28–30 (omitted in the Harvard Google Books scan between folios ``0056`` and ``0057``; reconstructed from Mascheroni's own internal citations across Books IV–XI)—Mascheroni completes the **240-part division** (:math:`1^\circ 30'`, § 57, using only three auxiliary points outside the circumference, cited in Book IX § 129) and solves **Problem 60: Bisect a given circular arc by compass alone** (cited throughout Books IV, VIII, and XI).
