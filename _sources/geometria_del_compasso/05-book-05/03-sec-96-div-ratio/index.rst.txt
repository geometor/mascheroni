=========================================
§ 96: Dividing a Segment in a Given Ratio
=========================================

.. folio:: 0074
   :page: 49

**§ 96. Problem (Fig. 40, folio 0074, p. 49).** Divide a given straight distance :math:`PQ` in the same ratio as a divided segment :math:`MP : PN`.

Mascheroni constructs the fourth proportional to :math:`MN`, :math:`MP`, and :math:`PQ` (§ 93) and transfers it collinearly onto :math:`(P, Q)` (§ 71), completing compass-only implementation of Euclid Book VI Prop. 10.
