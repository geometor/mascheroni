===============================================
§ 110: Line–Circle Intersection (Non-Diametral)
===============================================

.. folio:: 0085
   :page: 60

**§ 110. Problem (Figs. 46–47, folio 0085–folio 0086, pp. 60–61).** Given a circle :g:`( C M )` of center :g:`C` and radius :math:`r`, and a :term:`virtual line <retta-virtuale>` defined by two points :g:`A`, :g:`B` not collinear with :g:`C`, find the exact intersection points :g:`M`, :g:`N` of the line and circle using the compass alone.

*Construction:* Reflect center :g:`C` orthogonally across virtual line :math:`(A, B)` via Book IV § 78 by sweeping arcs :g:`( A C )` and :g:`( B C )` to obtain the mirror center :g:`c`. With center :g:`c` and the same radius :math:`r`, sweep the reflected circle :g:`( c M )`. Because :math:`(A, B)` is the perpendicular bisector of :g:`/ C c /` (§ 14), the two equal circles :g:`( C M )` and :g:`( c M )` intersect precisely at :g:`M` and :g:`N` on :math:`(A, B)`—solving line–circle intersection in just **3 circles** (:math:`8 C_1 + 3 C_3`, :math:`S = 11`)!
