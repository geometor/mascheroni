===============================================
§ 110: Line–Circle Intersection (Non-Diametral)
===============================================

.. folio:: 0085
   :page: 60

**BOOK SEVEN**

**ON THE INTERSECTION OF STRAIGHT LINES WITH CIRCULAR ARCS AND WITH ONE ANOTHER**

**110.** Given two points :math:`L` and :math:`M` (fig. 46) of a straight line, and the center :math:`A` with radius :math:`AB` of an arc :math:`BCD`: to find the two points :math:`P` and :math:`Q`, in which :math:`LM` cuts the said arc, if indeed it cuts it.

*Solution.* With the two points :math:`L` and :math:`M` given on the straight line taken as centers, and with their respective distances :math:`MA`, :math:`LA` from the given center taken as radii, describe two arcs intersecting at :math:`V`. With center :math:`V` and the given radius :math:`AB`, describe an indefinite arc :math:`EFG`. If this cuts the given arc at :math:`P` and :math:`Q`, these two points will be those sought. If it should not cut it, neither would the straight line :math:`LM` cut the given arc.

*Demonstration.* Since the four distances :math:`AP`, :math:`AQ`, :math:`VP`, :math:`VQ` are equal to one another, and the two :math:`AM`, :math:`VM` are equal to each other, the three points :math:`P, M, Q` will lie on the same straight line (§ 13). In the same manner it is demonstrated that the three points :math:`Q, P, L` lie on the same straight line. Hence the straight line :math:`LM` passes through :math:`P` and :math:`Q`, whenever these points of intersection exist.

If the circle :math:`EFG` described with radius :math:`AB` (fig. 47) and center :math:`V` should not cut the circle :math:`BCD`, :math:`LM` would not cut this same circle.
For if we conceive the straight line :math:`VA` cutting the circles at :math:`C` and :math:`F`, and :math:`CF` bisected at :math:`m`, we shall have :math:`Vm = mA`. Therefore :math:`LM` will cut :math:`VA` perpendicularly at :math:`m` (§ 14) outside the circle :math:`BCD`.
If any other point :math:`P` is taken on the straight line :math:`LM`, in the right triangle :math:`PmA` the side :math:`PA` will be greater than :math:`mA`, because opposite to a greater angle (I.32 and 18). Therefore point :math:`P` will lie much further outside the circle than point :math:`m`. Hence at no point will the straight line :math:`LM` cut the circle :math:`BCD`.
