===========================================
§§ 6–14: Compass Notation & Symmetry Lemmas
===========================================

.. folio:: 0042
   :page: 13

**§§ 6–10.** Mascheroni introduces compact verbal notation for compass sweeps (*«col centro A, raggio AB si tagli l'arco in C»*, *«si faccia ad AB = BC = CD»*) and establishes how circles of distinct apertures transfer distances without disturbing previously marked loci.

**§§ 11–14 (Fig. 2).** Fundamental symmetry and orthogonality lemmas: whenever two points :math:`P, Q` are each equidistant from two reference points :math:`A, B` (i.e., :math:`PA = PB` and :math:`QA = QB`), all such equidistant points are **collinear** (§ 13) and their virtual line :math:`(P, Q)` cuts segment :math:`AB` **at right angles and at its midpoint** (§ 14). These two lemmas replace Euclid's straightedge collinearity and right-angle checks throughout the entire treatise.
