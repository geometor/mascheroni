=============================================
§§ 71–75: Addition & Subtraction of Distances
=============================================

.. folio:: 0065
   :page: 40

**§§ 71–75 (Figs. 24–25, folio 0065–folio 0066, pp. 40–41).** Given two independent distances :math:`AB` and :math:`CD`, Mascheroni transfers :math:`CD` collinearly onto virtual line :math:`(A, B)` to form the difference :math:`AB - CD` (§ 72), the sum :math:`AB + CD` (§ 73, Fig. 24), or the overshoot :math:`CD > AB` (§ 74, Fig. 25): draw circle :math:`B(CD)`, cut it symmetrically at :math:`E, F` with an arbitrary arc centered at :math:`A`, and bisect arc :math:`EF` via Book II § 60 to land exact collinear points :math:`G` (difference) and :math:`H` (sum).
