=============================================
§§ 80–81: Parallels Through an External Point
=============================================

.. folio:: 0068
   :page: 43

**§ 80. Problem (Fig. 29, folio 0068, p. 43).** Through a given external point :g:`C`, construct a virtual line :math:`(C, D)` parallel to :math:`(A, B)`.

*Construction:* With center :g:`B` and radius :g:`/ A C /`, sweep arc :g:`( B C )`; with center :g:`C` and radius :g:`/ A B /`, sweep arc :g:`( C D )`. Let them intersect at :g:`D` on the side completing parallelogram :math:`ABDC`. Because opposite sides are equal (:eq:`/ A B / = / C D /` and :eq:`/ A C / = / B D /`), :math:`(C, D) \parallel (A, B)` in just **2 circles** (:math:`6 C_1 + 2 C_3`, :math:`S = 8`)—outperforming standard Euclidean ruler-and-compass constructions!

**§ 81 (Fig. 30, folio 0069, p. 44).** Extends § 80 to place a parallel segment of any specified length through :math:`C`.
