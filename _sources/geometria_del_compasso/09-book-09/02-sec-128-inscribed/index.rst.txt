======================================================
§§ 128–134: Inscribed & Circumscribed Regular Polygons
======================================================

.. folio:: 0095
   :page: 70

**§§ 128–130 (folio 0095–folio 0096, pp. 70–71).** Direct inscription of all Euclidean regular polygons using the circle divisions of Book II (including up to the 240-gon using only three exterior compass centers, § 129).

**§§ 131–134 (Figs. 59–62, folio 0097–folio 0099, pp. 72–74).** Compass-only circumscription of the equilateral triangle (§ 131, Fig. 59), square (§ 132, Fig. 60 — simply sweeping radius :math:`AB` from the four quadrant vertices :math:`B, F, E, f`!), and any general :math:`n`-gon (§§ 133–134, Figs. 61–62) via Book I § 22.
