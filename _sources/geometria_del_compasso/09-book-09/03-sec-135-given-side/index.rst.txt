============================================
§§ 135–142: Regular Polygons on a Given Side
============================================

.. folio:: 0099
   :page: 74

**§§ 135–142 (Figs. 63–70, folio 0099–folio 0106, pp. 74–81).** Given a single prescribed side :math:`AB`, Mascheroni constructs the vertices of every classical regular polygon using the compass alone:

* **§ 135:** Equilateral triangle on :math:`AE` (direct from § 1).
* **§ 136:** Square :math:`ABTF` on :math:`AB` (direct from § 100 Fig. 43).
* **§ 137 (Fig. 63, folio 0099):** Regular **pentagon** on :math:`AB` via the Golden Section point :math:`b`.
* **§ 138 (Fig. 65, folio 0101):** Regular **hexagon** :math:`ABCDEF` on :math:`AB`.
* **§ 139 (Fig. 66, folio 0101–folio 0104):** Regular **octagon** on :math:`AB` (multiple solutions).
* **§§ 140–142 (Fig. 69, folio 0105–folio 0106):** Regular **decagon** (using Golden Ratio circumradius :math:`bE = \phi\,AB`), dodecagon, and general regular :math:`n`-gon on a given side.
