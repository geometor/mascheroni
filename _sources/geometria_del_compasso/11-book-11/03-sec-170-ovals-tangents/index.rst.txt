==============================================================
§§ 170–179: Four-Center Ovals, Tangents & Architectural Arches
==============================================================

.. folio:: 0124
   :page: 99

**§§ 170–179 (Figs. 86–88, folio 0124–folio 0130, pp. 99–105).** Addressing architects and vault builders, Mascheroni constructs exact :math:`C^1`-smooth **four-center ovals** (pseudo-ellipses / basket-handle arches, § 172, Fig. 86) on any prescribed major axis :math:`BE` and minor axis :math:`MN`: four centers :math:`P, Q, R, r` forming a rhombus with equilateral triangles :math:`\triangle BFP, \triangle PQR` guarantee collinear joins :math:`F, P, R` so the circular arcs kiss tangentially (Euclid III.13) with exact ratio :math:`\sqrt{3} + 1`.
