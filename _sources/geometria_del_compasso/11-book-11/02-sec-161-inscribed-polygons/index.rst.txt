======================================================
§§ 161–169: Inscribed Squares, Octagons & Area Scaling
======================================================

.. folio:: 0115
   :page: 90

**§ 161 (Fig. 79, folio 0115, p. 90).** Inscribe a square :math:`becd` inside a given equilateral triangle :math:`ABC` by compass alone. Using :math:`EC = \sqrt{3}`, Mascheroni obtains side :math:`be = 2\sqrt{3} - 3 = (2 - \sqrt{3})\sqrt{3}` directly along :math:`AB`.

**§§ 162–169 (Fig. 83, folio 0116–folio 0123, pp. 91–98).** Inscribing regular octagons inside squares (side :math:`\sqrt{2} - 1`), constructing regular octagons of **double** and **triple area** (§ 165, Fig. 83 — the widest chalkboard plate in the entire treatise!), and polygon area equivalence transformations.
