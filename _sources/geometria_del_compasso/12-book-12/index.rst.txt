===================================
Book XII: Problems by Approximation
===================================

.. rubric:: *Libro Duodecimo: Problemi per approssimazione (§§ 193–280)*

.. folio:: 0145
   :page: 120

Mascheroni's tour de force of applied precision geometry (§§ 193–280, pp. 120–151, folios ``0145``–``0176``): twelve chord equations for dividing circles into sexagesimal degrees, arc-minutes, and French 400-grad decimal divisions (§§ 193–266), followed by astonishingly accurate compass solutions to the classic transcendental and cubic problems — rectifying the quadrant (:math:`\pi/2`), squaring the circle (:math:`\sqrt{\pi}`), cubing the sphere, and duplicating through octuplicating the cube (:math:`\sqrt[3]{2}` to :math:`\sqrt[3]{8}`, reaching two-millionths accuracy in § 276 Sol. II!).
