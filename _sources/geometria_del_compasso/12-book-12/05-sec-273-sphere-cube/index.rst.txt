==============================================================
§§ 273–280: Sphere Cubature & Cube Duplication (∛2 through ∛8)
==============================================================

.. folio:: 0172
   :page: 147

**§§ 273–275 (Figs. 105–106, folio 0172–folio 0173, pp. 147–148).** **Cubature of the Sphere**: constructs the cube edge equal in volume to a unit sphere (:math:`PG = 1.6122696` vs. :math:`\sqrt[3]{\frac{4}{3}\pi} = 1.611991`, error :math:`< 0.0003`, § 273) and inversely the sphere radius equal in volume to a unit cube (:math:`LM = 0.6195986` vs. :math:`0.6203504`, error :math:`< 0.0008`, § 274).

**§ 276 (Fig. 107, folio 0173–folio 0174, pp. 148–149).** Duplication of the Cube (Delian Problem, :math:`\sqrt[3]{2}`): Solution I yields :math:`aN = 1.2592800` (error :math:`< 0.0007`), while **Solution II** delivers :math:`PQ = 1.2599190` against exact :math:`\sqrt[3]{2} = 1.2599209`—a breathtaking defect of barely two millionths of the radius (:math:`0.0000019`)!

**§§ 277–280 (Fig. 108, folio 0174–folio 0176, pp. 149–151).** Grand finale of *La Geometria del Compasso*: a single unified compass diagram producing simultaneous approximations for **duplication through octuplication of the cube** (:math:`\sqrt[3]{2}, \sqrt[3]{3}, \sqrt[3]{4}, \sqrt[3]{5}, \sqrt[3]{6}, \sqrt[3]{7}, \sqrt[3]{8}`), closing the treatise at § 280 (*Fine*).
