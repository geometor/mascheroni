========================================================
§§ 236–266: Arc-Minutes & French 400-Grad Decimal Circle
========================================================

.. folio:: 0159
   :page: 134

**§§ 236–249 (folio 0159–folio 0163, pp. 134–138).** Step-by-step compass recipes for dividing astronomical limbs down to :math:`1^\circ`, :math:`10'`, :math:`5'`, and :math:`1'`—for example § 249 (folio 0163) yields an arc of :math:`5'` with error less than :math:`1'''` (:math:`\frac{1}{60}` of an arc-second)!

**§§ 250–266 (folio 0163–folio 0167, pp. 138–142).** Writing in Year V of the French Republic (1797)—just one year before being summoned to Paris for the Metric Commission—Mascheroni adapts all twelve equations to the revolutionary **French decimal (centesimal) division of the circle into 400 grads** (:math:`100` grads per quadrant, :math:`100` decimal minutes per grad, noting :math:`9^\circ = 10\text{ grads}`).
