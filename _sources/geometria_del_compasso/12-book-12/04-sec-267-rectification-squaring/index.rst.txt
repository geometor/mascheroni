============================================================
§§ 267–272: Circle Rectification, Radian Arc & Squaring (√π)
============================================================

.. folio:: 0168
   :page: 143

**§§ 267–268. Problem (Fig. 101, folio 0168, p. 143).** Find a chord :g:`/ B b /` approximately equal to one quarter of the circumference (:math:`\pi/2`) by compass alone.

*Construction:* On unit circle :g:`( A B )`, step :math:`AB = BC = CD = DE = 1` (:g:`B`, :g:`C`, :g:`D`, :g:`E`), intersect :g:`( B D )` :math:`\cap` :g:`( E C )` :math:`=` :g:`a` (:math:`Aa = \sqrt{2}`), and with center :g:`C` and radius :g:`/ C a /` sweep arc :g:`( C a )` cutting the circle at :g:`b` (:eq:`/ C a / = / C b /`). Then chord :g:`/ B b /` :math:`= 1.5711996\dots` versus :math:`\pi/2 = 1.5707963\dots`—an error of just :math:`0.0004`, more accurate than Archimedes' famous :math:`11/7 \approx 1.5714` (§ 268) in barely five arcs!

**§ 269 (Fig. 102, folio 0169, p. 144).** Constructs the **1-radian arc** :math:`LM = 57^\circ 17' 43''+` (within :math:`1''` of exact :math:`57^\circ 17' 44''`).

**§§ 270–272 (Figs. 103–104, folio 0169–folio 0171, pp. 144–146).** **Squaring the Circle** by compass alone: yields square side :math:`BR = 1.7726566` vs. :math:`\sqrt{\pi} = 1.7724538` (error :math:`\approx 0.0002`!).
