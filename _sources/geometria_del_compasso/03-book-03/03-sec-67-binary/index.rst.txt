=================================
§ 67: Repeated Binary Subdivision
=================================

.. folio:: 0059
   :page: 34

**§ 67. Problem (Fig. 19).** Divide a given distance :math:`AB` into 4, 8, 16, or any power of two (:math:`2^k`) equal parts using the compass alone.

Mascheroni presents three solutions (pp. 34–35, folio 0059–folio 0060) showing how nested similar isosceles triangles share a single doubled anchor line rather than restarting § 66 from scratch at each halving step, dramatically lowering the Lemoine simplicity cost :math:`S` for dyadic scales.
