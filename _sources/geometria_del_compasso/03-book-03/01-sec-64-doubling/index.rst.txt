================================================
§§ 64–65: Collinear Doubling & Integer Multiples
================================================

.. folio:: 0057
   :page: 32

**§ 64. Problem.** Given two points :g:`B` and :g:`A`, find a third point :g:`E` on the virtual line :math:`(B, A)` such that :eq:`/ A E / = / B A /` (hence :math:`BE = 2\,AB`).

*Construction:* With center :g:`A` and radius :g:`/ B A /`, describe circle :g:`( A B )`. Using the same compass opening :g:`/ B A /`, step three successive chords around the circle from :g:`B`: :g:`( B C )` locating :g:`C`, :g:`( C D )` locating :g:`D`, and :g:`( D E )` locating :g:`E` (:eq:`/ B C / = / C D / = / D E /`). Because three inscribed equilateral triangles span :math:`3 \times 60^\circ = 180^\circ`, :g:`B`, :g:`A`, :g:`E` are strictly collinear and :math:`BE = 2\,AB`.

**§ 65.** Repeating :term:`collinear doubling <raddoppiamento>` iteratively generates any integer multiple :math:`n \cdot AB` along the virtual line without ever using a straightedge.
