=================================================
§§ 69–70: Division into Any Number of Equal Parts
=================================================

.. folio:: 0062
   :page: 37

**§ 69. Problem (Figs. 21–23).** Divide a given straight distance :math:`AB` into any prescribed integer number :math:`n` of equal parts using the compass alone.

Generalizing § 66 (:math:`n=2`) and § 68 (:math:`n=3`), Mascheroni extends :math:`AB` collinearly to :math:`n \cdot AB` (§ 65), intersects :math:`V(n)` with :math:`A(1)` (or optimized composite factors as illustrated for :math:`n=5` on folio 0063), and reflects via similar isosceles triangles to obtain exact length :math:`\frac{1}{n}AB`.

**§ 70 (p. 39, folio 0064).** Concluding remark on minimizing acute intersection angles when :math:`n` is large by factoring :math:`n` or choosing an auxiliary enlarged base.
