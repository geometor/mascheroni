=========================================
§ 68: Exact Distance Trisection — Summary
=========================================

.. folio:: 0061
   :page: 36

Exact segment trisection (§ 68, Fig. 20): bilateral extension :math:`EV = 3\,AB`, intersection :math:`V(3) \cap E(2) = \{F, f\}`, and reflection :math:`F(2) \cap f(2) = T` yielding :math:`AT = \frac{1}{3}AB` identically.
