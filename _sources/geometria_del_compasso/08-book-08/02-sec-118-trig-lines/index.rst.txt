=================================================
§§ 118–123: Angle Bisection & Trigonometric Lines
=================================================

.. folio:: 0090
   :page: 65

**§ 118 (Fig. 54, folio 0090, p. 65).** Bisect an angle :math:`\angle BAC` given only by three points :math:`B, A, C` with :math:`AB \neq AC`. Sweep :math:`A(AB)`, copy :math:`CB = CD` to double the angle to :math:`\angle BAD`, then bisect arc :math:`BMD` at :math:`M` and arc :math:`BM` at :math:`N` (§ 60).

**§§ 119–123 (Figs. 55–57, folio 0090–folio 0093, pp. 65–68).** Complete compass trigonometry: given arc :math:`BC` of center :math:`A`, construct its **sine** :math:`CM`, **cosine** :math:`MA`, **tangent** :math:`BS`, and **secant** :math:`SA` (§ 119, using third proportional :math:`AM : AC :: AB : AS`); conversely, recover the exact arc :math:`BC` from its given sine (§ 120, Fig. 56), cosine (§ 121, Fig. 57), tangent (§ 122), or secant (§ 123).
