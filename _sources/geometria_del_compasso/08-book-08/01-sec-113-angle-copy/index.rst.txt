=================================================
§§ 113–117: Copying, Multiplying & Testing Angles
=================================================

.. folio:: 0088
   :page: 63

**§§ 113–115 (Fig. 51, folio 0088–folio 0089, pp. 63–64).** Given three points :math:`B, A, C` defining an angle :math:`\angle BAC` (with sides not drawn), copy :math:`\angle BAC` onto a new vertex and ray (and construct a similar triangle :math:`abc` on given side :math:`ab`, § 115) by scaling chords proportionally via Book V § 93.

**§§ 116–117 (Figs. 52–53, folio 0089, p. 64).** Step chords :math:`CB = CD = DE = EF` on concentric arcs :math:`A(AB)` and :math:`A(AC)` to double, triple, or quadruple any angle (§ 116); test whether :math:`\angle BAG = 45^\circ` by doubling it to :math:`\angle BAF` and testing whether :math:`BF = FE` on the semicircle (§ 117).
