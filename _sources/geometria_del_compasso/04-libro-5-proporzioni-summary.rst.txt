2. Exact Ratio Conservation
---------------------------

By using concentric circles centered at an arbitrary origin :math:`O`, Mascheroni creates an angle-preserving dilation (homothety) centered at :math:`O`:

.. math::

   h_{O, b/a} : \Gamma_a \to \Gamma_b, \qquad \overline{DE} = \frac{b}{a} \overline{BC}.

Because rotation about :math:`O` is an isometry, transferring chord :math:`BC` onto :math:`\Gamma_b` requires only compass radius setting, eliminating the entire machinery of parallel transversals!
