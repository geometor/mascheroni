==============================================
Fazzari on Geometrography & Legacy (pp. X–XVI)
==============================================

.. folio:: 0023
   :page: X

Continuing his centenary introduction (pp. X–XVI), :person:`Gaetano Fazzari <fazzari>` recounts General :person:`Napoléon Bonaparte <bonaparte>`'s celebrated exchange in Paris with :person:`Pierre-Simon Laplace <laplace>` and :person:`Joseph-Louis Lagrange <lagrange>`, where Bonaparte posed Mascheroni's compass-only circle quadrisection (§ 27) and center-finding (§ 143) problems, prompting Laplace's famous quip: *«Nous attendions tout de vous, général, excepté des leçons de géométrie.»*

On page XI (folio 0024), Fazzari introduces a critical methodological judgment connecting Mascheroni directly to :person:`Émile Lemoine <lemoine>`'s :term:`Géométrographie Mascheronienne <geometrographie-mascheronienne>`: while Mascheroni's proofs rely heavily on classical references to :person:`Euclid <euclid>`, his actual compass-only constructions are often demonstrably simpler and more accurate under Lemoine's operational calculus (:math:`m_1 C_1 + m_2 C_2 + m_3 C_3`) than standard ruler-and-compass constructions. Fazzari concludes with Mascheroni's delegation to the Paris Metric Commission and his death in Paris on 14 July 1800.
