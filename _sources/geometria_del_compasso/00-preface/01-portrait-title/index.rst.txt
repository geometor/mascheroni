===============================
Portrait & Centenary Title Page
===============================

.. folio:: 0013
   :page: frontispiece

The archival witness digitized for this GEOMETOR Scriptorium edition is the centenary reprint edited by :person:`Gaetano Fazzari <fazzari>`, *La Geometria del Compasso (Nuova edizione)* (Palermo: Premiata Casa Editrice «Era Nova», 1901; Harvard Science Center Library copy ``Math 5409.01``), reproducing :person:`Lorenzo Mascheroni <mascheroni>`'s original 1797 Pavia treatise (*Presso gli Eredi di Pietro Galeazzi*, Anno V) together with all twelve books, sections §§ 1–280, and 108 inline chalkboard figures.

The frontispiece (folio 0013) carries an engraved profile medallion of Mascheroni (1750–1800), followed by the centenary title page (folio 0014) celebrating the hundredth anniversary of his death in Paris.
