Lagrange
========

**Joseph-Louis Lagrange** (1736 – 1813) — *Italian-born French Analyst & Academician*
   Examined Mascheroni's methods presented by Bonaparte at the Institut National in Paris and recognized the foundational constructive power of pure compass arcs.
