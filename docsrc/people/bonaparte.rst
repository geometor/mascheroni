Bonaparte
=========

**Napoléon Bonaparte** (1769 – 1821) — *General, French Statesman & Member of the Institut National*
   Met Lorenzo Mascheroni in Lombardy in 1797 during the Italian campaign and received the poetic dedication of *La Geometria del Compasso*. Upon returning to Paris in December 1797, Bonaparte astonished Laplace and Lagrange at the Institut National by demonstrating Mascheroni's compass-only circle quadrisection, provoking Laplace's celebrated compliment: *"Nous attendions tout de vous, général, excepté des leçons de géométrie."*
