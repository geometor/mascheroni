Steiner
=======

**Jakob Steiner** (1796 – 1863) — *Swiss Synthetic Projective Geometer & Professor in Berlin*
   Proved the dual minimal-instrument theorem in 1833 (*Die geometrischen Constructionen, ausgeführt mittelst der geraden Linie und Eines festen Kreises*): the straightedge *alone* suffices for all Euclidean constructions, provided a single fixed circle and its center are given in the plane.
