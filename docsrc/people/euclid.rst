Euclid
======

**Euclid of Alexandria** (fl. c. 300 BCE) — *Alexandrian Mathematician & Author of the Elements*
   Author of the *Elements*, whose first three constructive postulates define Euclidean plane geometry. Mascheroni demonstrated that Postulates 1 and 2 (drawing and extending continuous straight lines) are redundant for determining all geometric points: Postulate 3 (compass circle sweep) alone suffices.
