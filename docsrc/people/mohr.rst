Mohr
====

**Georg Mohr** (1640 – 1697) — *Danish Mathematician & Author of Euclides Danicus (1672)*
   Published *Euclides Danicus* in Dutch and Danish at Amsterdam in 1672—125 years before Mascheroni—proving that all constructions of Euclid Books I–VI can be executed with compass alone. The treatise remained entirely overlooked as an optical work until :person:`Johannes Hjelmslev <hjelmslev>` rediscovered and republished it in 1928. The theorem is thus known today as the **Mohr–Mascheroni Theorem**.
