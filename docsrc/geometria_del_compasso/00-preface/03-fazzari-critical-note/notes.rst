Witness & Folio Provenance
--------------------------

* **Archival Witness:** :cite:`fazzari-1901` (Palermo 1901 centenary reprint of :cite:`mascheroni-1797`)
* **Folio Span:** ``0023``–``0029`` (printed pp. ``X``–``XVI``)

Folios ``0023``–``0029`` correspond to Roman pages ``X``–``XVI`` of :cite:`fazzari-1901`. Writing in 1901, Fazzari did not yet know of :person:`Georg Mohr <mohr>`'s *Euclides Danicus* (1672), which :person:`Johannes Hjelmslev <hjelmslev>` rediscovered in Copenhagen in 1928 (:cite:`mohr-1672`).
