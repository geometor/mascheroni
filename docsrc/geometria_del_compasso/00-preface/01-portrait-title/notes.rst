Portrait & Centenary Title Page — Critical Notes
================================================

Witness & Folio Provenance
--------------------------

* **Archival Witness:** :cite:`fazzari-1901` (Palermo 1901 centenary reprint of :cite:`mascheroni-1797`)
* **Folio Span:** ``0001``–``0015`` (printed pp. ``cover``–``title-verso``)

This leaf accounts for the complete binding and frontmatter sequence of the digitized Harvard copy (:cite:`fazzari-1901`): Google Books cover (``0001``), Harvard bookplate (``0003``), flyleaves (``0004``–``0012``), medallion portrait (``0013``), title page (``0014``), and Harvard acquisition stamp dated 10 February 1903 (``0015``). Across the entire witness, 181 300-DPI scans preserve every page except a four-page scanner lacuna (printed pp. 28–31) between folios ``0056`` and ``0057``.
