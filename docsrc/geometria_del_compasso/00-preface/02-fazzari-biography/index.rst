===========================================
Fazzari's Biographical Preface (pp. III–IX)
===========================================

.. folio:: 0016
   :page: III

In the opening half of his historical preface (*Lorenzo Mascheroni e le sue opere matematiche*, pp. III–IX), :person:`Gaetano Fazzari <fazzari>` traces :person:`Lorenzo Mascheroni <mascheroni>`'s formation in Bergamo (born 13 May 1750 at Castagneta), his early literary studies, his appointment to the chair of algebra and geometry at the University of Pavia (1786), and his seminal *Adnotationes ad calculum integralem Euleri* (1790), which introduced the Euler–Mascheroni constant :math:`\gamma` to thirty-two decimal places.

Fazzari highlights the rare synthesis in Mascheroni of Italian poetic elegance (*Invito a Lesbia Cidonia*, 1793) and rigorous constructive invention, setting the stage for the birth of :term:`La Geometria del Compasso <geometria-del-compasso>` in 1797.
