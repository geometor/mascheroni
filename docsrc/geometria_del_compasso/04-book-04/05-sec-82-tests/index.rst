====================================================
§§ 82–85: Collinearity, Right-Angle & Parallel Tests
====================================================

.. folio:: 0069
   :page: 44

**82.** *Prob.* To examine whether the three points :math:`A, B, C` lie in a straight line (fig. 31).

.. picture:: 0069.g2.png
   :alt: Archival Fig. 31 (folio 0069, p. 44): Testing whether three points A, B, C are collinear (§ 82)

*Sol.* With centers :math:`A` and :math:`C` and an arbitrary radius, for instance :math:`AC`, mark two arcs intersecting at :math:`D` and :math:`E`. Observe whether :math:`DB = ED`. If so, the three points :math:`A, B, C` are in a straight line; otherwise not.

*Dem.* If :math:`DB = EB` as well, then angle :math:`DAB = EAB = \frac{1}{2} DAE` (I.8), by reason of the equal sides in the two triangles :math:`DAB, EAB`. But in triangles :math:`DAC, EAC` for the same reason angles :math:`DAC, EAC` are equal, and therefore each is equal to half the angle :math:`DAE`. Hence :math:`DAB = DAC`, and thus the three points :math:`A, B, C` lie in a straight line.
If however :math:`DB` be greater or less than :math:`EB`, angle :math:`DAB` will also be greater or less than angle :math:`EAB` (I.25), and hence cannot be equal to angle :math:`DAC`, since it cannot be equal

.. folio:: 0070
   :page: 45

to half the angle :math:`DAE`. Therefore the three points :math:`A, B, C` cannot lie in a straight line.

**83.** *Prob.* Given the three points :math:`A, B, D`, to examine whether :math:`DA` is perpendicular to :math:`AB` (fig. 32).

.. picture:: 0070.g1.png
   :alt: Archival Fig. 32 (folio 0070, p. 45): Testing whether angle DAB is right (§ 83)

*Sol.* Double :math:`AB` at :math:`E` (§ 64) by means of the semicircle :math:`BPQE`. Observe whether :math:`DB = DE`. If it is, the angle :math:`DAB` is right; otherwise it is not.

*Dem.* Since the diameter :math:`BAE` of the circle is a straight line, :math:`DA` will make with it two angles whose sum is equal to two right angles (I.13).
Then in the two triangles :math:`DAE, DAB`, which have sides :math:`AE, AB` mutually equal and side :math:`AD` common, if side :math:`DE` be equal to side :math:`DB`, angle :math:`DAE` will also be equal to angle :math:`DAB` (I.8), and therefore both right angles. If however :math:`DE` be greater or less than :math:`DB`, angle :math:`DAE` will also be greater or less than angle :math:`DAB`; hence one will be acute and the other obtuse (I.25).

**84.** *Prob.* To examine whether the straight line passing through two given points :math:`D, F` (fig. 33) is perpendicular to the straight line passing through two other given points :math:`A, B`.

.. picture:: 0070.g2.png
   :alt: Archival Fig. 33 (folio 0070, p. 45): Testing perpendicularity of two point-pairs (§ 84)

*Sol.* By means of point :math:`D`, find the perpendicular :math:`DE` to :math:`AB` (§ 78). Examine whether the three points :math:`D, E, F` lie in a straight line (§ 82). If so, :math:`DF` is perpendicular to :math:`AB`; otherwise not.

*Dem.* Since :math:`DE` is perpendicular by construction, if :math:`DF` is so also, it will be the same straight line; because from a point :math:`D` to a straight line :math:`AB` two perpendiculars cannot be drawn (Coroll. I.32).

**85.** *Prob.* Given two points :math:`A, B` of one straight line (fig. 34) and two :math:`C, D` of another, to examine whether they are parallel.

*Sol.* Make :math:`AD = AE`, and :math:`BD = BE` (§ 11). Likewise make :math:`AC = AF`, and :math:`BC = BF`. Observe whether :math:`DE = CF`;

.. folio:: 0071
   :page: 46

if so, they will be parallel; if not, they will converge toward the lesser side.

.. picture:: 0071.g1.png
   :alt: Archival Fig. 34 (folio 0071, p. 46): Testing parallelism of two virtual lines (§ 85)

*Dem.* The lines :math:`DE, CF` are perpendicular to :math:`AB`, and are bisected by it at two points :math:`M` and :math:`N` (§ 14). Therefore they are double respectively of the distances :math:`DM, CN` of the points :math:`D` and :math:`C` from the straight line :math:`AB`. If then they are equal, the distances will be equal, and hence :math:`AB, DC` parallel; otherwise they will converge.
