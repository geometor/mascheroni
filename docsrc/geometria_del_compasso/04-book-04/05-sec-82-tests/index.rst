====================================================
§§ 82–85: Collinearity, Right-Angle & Parallel Tests
====================================================

.. folio:: 0069
   :page: 44

**§§ 82–85 (Figs. 31–34, folio 0069–folio 0071, pp. 44–46).** Concluding Book IV, Mascheroni supplies four pure-compass *decision procedures* replacing visual straightedge checks:

* **§ 82 (Fig. 31):** Test whether three points :math:`A, B, C` are collinear by doubling or reflecting across :math:`(A, B)`.
* **§ 83 (Fig. 32):** Test whether :math:`DA \perp AB` by doubling :math:`AB` to :math:`E` (§ 64) and checking with the compass whether :math:`DB = DE`.
* **§ 84 (Fig. 33):** Test whether virtual line :math:`(D, F)` is perpendicular to :math:`(A, B)`.
* **§ 85 (Fig. 34):** Test whether virtual lines :math:`(A, B)` and :math:`(C, D)` are parallel by reflecting :math:`C \to F` and :math:`D \to E` across :math:`(A, B)` and checking :math:`DE = CF`.
