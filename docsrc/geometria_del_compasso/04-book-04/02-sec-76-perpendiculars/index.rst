=================================================
§§ 76–77: Erecting a Perpendicular at an Endpoint
=================================================

.. folio:: 0067
   :page: 42

**§ 76. Problem (Fig. 26, folio 0067, p. 42).** At a given point :math:`B` of virtual line :math:`(A, B)`, erect a perpendicular without using a straightedge.

*Construction:* Take an arbitrary point :math:`C` not on :math:`(A, B)` and draw circle :math:`C(CB)` cutting :math:`A(AC)`—or simply double :math:`AB` past :math:`B` to :math:`E` via § 64 and intersect equal arcs :math:`A(r) \cap E(r) = P` so that :math:`PB \perp AB` by Book I § 14. In § 77 (Fig. 27, folio 0068, p. 43), Mascheroni constructs a perpendicular at :math:`B` having a **prescribed length** :math:`CD`.
