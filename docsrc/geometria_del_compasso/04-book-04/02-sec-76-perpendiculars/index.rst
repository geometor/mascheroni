=================================================
§§ 76–77: Erecting a Perpendicular at an Endpoint
=================================================

.. folio:: 0067
   :page: 42

**76.** *Prob.* Given the two points :math:`A` and :math:`B`, to find a point :math:`H` such that the straight line :math:`BH` is perpendicular to :math:`AB` at :math:`B` and equal to a given distance :math:`CD` (fig. 26).

.. picture:: 0067.g1.png
   :alt: Archival Fig. 26 (folio 0067, p. 42): Erecting a perpendicular at endpoint B via semicircle doubling

*Sol.* With center :math:`B` and distance :math:`CD` as radius, describe a circle :math:`FEHG`. With center :math:`A` and distance :math:`AB` as radius,
describe an arc cutting the circle at :math:`F` and :math:`E`. Determine the semicircumference :math:`FEG` (§ 64). Bisect the arc :math:`GE` at :math:`H` (§ 60). Then :math:`H` will be the point sought.

*Dem.* By the similarity of the two triangles :math:`GEB, EBA` (§ 22), angle :math:`GEB = EBA`, and hence :math:`GE` is parallel to :math:`BA` (I.28).
But :math:`BH`, which bisects the angle :math:`GBE`, is perpendicular to the chord :math:`GE` (I.9, 11, 12). Therefore it is also perpendicular to :math:`BA` (I.28). Moreover :math:`BH = CD`. Hence :math:`H` is the point sought.

If :math:`AB` should be too much less than :math:`CD`, double or triple it, etc. (§§ 64, 65).

**77.** *Prob.* Given the two points :math:`A` and :math:`B`, to find a point :math:`D` such that :math:`DA` is perpendicular to :math:`AB` (fig. 27).

*Sol.* Taking an arbitrary radius (for example :math:`AB` itself),

.. folio:: 0068
   :page: 43

with it and with the two centers :math:`A` and :math:`B`, describe two arcs intersecting at :math:`C`. With this same radius and center :math:`C`, describe the semicircumference :math:`BAD` (§ 64). Then :math:`D` will be the point sought.

.. picture:: 0068.g1.png
   :alt: Archival Fig. 27 (folio 0068, p. 43): Perpendicular of prescribed length at an endpoint

*Dem.* The angle :math:`DAB` is in a semicircle. Therefore it is a right angle (III.31).
