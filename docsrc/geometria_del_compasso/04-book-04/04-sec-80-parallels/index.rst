=============================================
§§ 80–81: Parallels Through an External Point
=============================================

.. folio:: 0068
   :page: 43

**80.** *Prob.* Given two points :g:`A` and :g:`B` of a straight line, and a point :g:`C` outside it (fig. 29), through which it is desired to draw a parallel to :math:`AB`; to find another point :g:`D` determining its position.

.. picture:: 0068.g3.svg
   :alt: Calibrated 5-layer vector diagram of Book IV § 80 (Fig. 29): parallel (C, D) || (A, B) in 2 circles (6C₁ + 2C₃)

.. picture:: 0068.g3.png
   :alt: Archival Fig. 29 (folio 0068, p. 43): Two-circle parallelogram parallel construction

*Sol.* Make :math:`CA = BD` (§ 11); :math:`BA = CD`. Then :g:`D` will be the point sought.

*Dem.* In the two triangles :math:`CDB, CAB`, which have their three sides equal to one another, angle :math:`DCB` is equal to angle :math:`CBA` (I.8). Therefore :math:`CD` is parallel to :math:`AB` (I.28).

**81.** *Prob.* Given two points :math:`A` and :math:`B` of a straight line, and a point

.. folio:: 0069
   :page: 44

:math:`C` outside it, to place at this point :math:`C` a distance :math:`CE` (fig. 30) such that the straight line :math:`CE` is parallel to :math:`AB` and equal to a given :math:`MN`.

.. picture:: 0069.g1.svg
   :alt: Calibrated 5-layer vector diagram of Mascheroni Book IV § 81 (Fig. 30): Parallel Lines and Equal Segments

.. picture:: 0069.g1.png
   :alt: Archival Fig. 30 (folio 0069, p. 44): Parallel at a prescribed length

*Sol.* Having found a point :math:`D` of the parallel passing through :math:`C` by the method of the preceding Problem (§ 80), upon the direction of this :math:`CD` lay off :math:`MN`
by subtracting it from :math:`CD` if it is smaller (§ 72), or adding it on the other side (§ 73), or laying it off upon :math:`CD` from :math:`C` toward :math:`D` if it is greater (§ 74), according as the conditions of the Problem require.

This Solution has no need of demonstration.
