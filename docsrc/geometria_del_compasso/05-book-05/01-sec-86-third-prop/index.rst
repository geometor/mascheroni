=================================================
§§ 86–92: Third Proportional & Iterative Doubling
=================================================

.. folio:: 0071
   :page: 46

**§§ 86–92 (Figs. 35–38, folio 0071–folio 0073, pp. 46–48).** Given two segments :math:`Qp` and :math:`MN`, find their **third proportional** :math:`x = (MN)^2 / Qp` using the compass alone.

When :math:`2\,Qp > MN` (§§ 86–87, Figs. 35–36), circles :math:`Q(Qp)` and :math:`p(MN)` intersect directly, yielding the third proportional via Book I § 22. When :math:`Qp` is too small for direct intersection (§§ 88–92, Figs. 37–38), Mascheroni doubles :math:`pQ` collinearly to :math:`Q', Q'', \dots` (§ 64) until the enlarged circle intersects :math:`p(MN)`, and then scales the resulting chord by the matching power of 2—a completely general, inversion-equivalent engine that later solves Book X § 143!
