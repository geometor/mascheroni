============================================================
§§ 108–109: Acute-Angle Refinement & Alternative Root Scheme
============================================================

.. folio:: 0083
   :page: 58

**§§ 108–109 (pp. 58–60, folio 0083–folio 0085).** Demonstrating the practical sensibility of an experimental physicist, Mascheroni observes that minimizing compass changes in § 104 produces a few slightly acute arc intersections (notably at points :math:`S, s, O, o, L, l`). He therefore supplies an alternative construction of Fig. 45 (§ 109) whose intersections meet much closer to :math:`90^\circ`, proving via Apollonius's median theorem (Book I § 26) that both schemes determine the exact same radical points.
