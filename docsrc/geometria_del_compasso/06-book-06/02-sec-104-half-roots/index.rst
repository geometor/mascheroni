=======================================
§§ 104–107: Half-Roots ½√1 through ½√25
=======================================

.. folio:: 0078
   :page: 53

**§§ 104–107 (Fig. 45, folio 0078–folio 0082, pp. 53–57).** Mascheroni constructs a single remarkably compact figure inside and around the unit circle that delivers all twenty-five half-roots :math:`\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{1}, \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}, \dots, \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{25}` using only the three principal compass openings (:math:`1`, :math:`\sqrt{3}`, and :math:`2`).

Because ratios of half-roots equal ratios of full roots, Book IX (§ 126) uses Fig. 45 directly to scale the areas of arbitrary polygons in any rational proportion :math:`p : q`.
