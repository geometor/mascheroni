===============================================
§§ 23–26: Parallelograms & Apollonius's Theorem
===============================================

.. folio:: 0046
   :page: 17

**§§ 23–25 (Figs. 5–6).** Mascheroni establishes the metric laws of symmetric trapezoids (:math:`\pi\tau \cdot BD = (BD)^2 - (B\pi)^2`, § 23) and parallelograms (sum of squares of the diagonals equals sum of squares of the four sides, § 25).

**§ 26 (Fig. 7).** Apollonius's median theorem: in any triangle with base bisected at :math:`T`, :math:`(HL)^2 + (LR)^2 = 2(HT)^2 + 2(TL)^2`. Mascheroni closes Book I with this identity, which reappears in Book VI (§ 109) and Book XII (§ 277) to prove exact equality of alternative compass constructions.
