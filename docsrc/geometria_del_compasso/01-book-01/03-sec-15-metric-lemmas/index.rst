=============================================
§§ 15–22: Isosceles & Chord Metric Identities
=============================================

.. folio:: 0045
   :page: 16

**§§ 15–20 (Fig. 3).** Mascheroni derives exact quadratic distance identities for a point :math:`Q` collinear with the base :math:`pP` of an isosceles triangle :math:`ApP`: :math:`(AQ)^2 = (Ap)^2 + pQ \cdot PQ` (external) and :math:`(AQ)^2 = (Ap)^2 - pQ \cdot pP` (internal). These algebraic identities—direct compass-age counterparts of Stewart's theorem—allow Mascheroni to compute exact radical lengths such as :math:`\sqrt{7}` in Book VI (§ 100) and exact trig-chord equations in Book XI.

**§§ 21–22 (Fig. 4).** Proportional chords in similar isosceles triangles: whenever :math:`pP \cdot VC = (CD)^2`, the base segments stand in exact third proportion—the very identity that drives Book V (§§ 86–92) and Book X (§ 143).
