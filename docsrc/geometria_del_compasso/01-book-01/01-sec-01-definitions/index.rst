===================================================
§§ 1–5: Principles & Fundamental Compass Operations
===================================================

.. folio:: 0040
   :page: 11

**§ 1.** :person:`Lorenzo Mascheroni <mascheroni>` establishes the sole constructive primitive of the treatise: given two points :g:`A` and :g:`E`, the compass sweeps circles :g:`( A E )` and :g:`( E A )` of radius :g:`/ A E /`, intersecting at :g:`D` and :g:`d` so that :eq:`/ A E / = / A D / = / E D /`. Every straight line in the treatise is treated purely as a :term:`virtual line <retta-virtuale>` determined by two constructed points—never drawn by a ruler.

**§§ 2–5.** Stepping radius :g:`/ A E /` around a circle produces the regular inscribed hexagon and the diagonal chord :math:`\sqrt{3}` (:math:`BD = \sqrt{3}\,AB`), together with the elementary congruence relations of equilateral and isosceles triangles that underwrite all later circle-stepping algorithms.
