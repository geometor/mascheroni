=========================================
§§ 124–127: Similar Figures & Area Ratios
=========================================

.. folio:: 0093
   :page: 68

**§§ 124–127 (Fig. 58, folio 0093–folio 0095, pp. 68–70).** Mascheroni defines a polygon in compass-only geometry solely by its ordered vertex set (§ 124) and shows how to construct a similar figure whose area bears any prescribed rational ratio :math:`p : q` (illustrated by :math:`2 : 5` on folio 0095) to a given figure: draw two concentric circles of radii :math:`\sqrt{p}` and :math:`\sqrt{q}` taken straight from Book VI's half-root lattice (Fig. 45, § 104), and scale every triangulation side by Book V § 93.
