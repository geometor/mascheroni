=====================================================
Notes: Notation & Fundamental Collinearity Lemma
=====================================================

.. rubric:: Orthogonal Reflection and the Compass Transformation Group

.. folio:: 0043

Reflection as Primitive Involution
----------------------------------

Mascheroni's Lemma (§ 13) provides the foundational engine for all subsequent constructions in *La Geometria del Compasso*:
* Given two points :math:`A, B` defining a virtual axis :math:`L`, and an external point :math:`P`, the point :math:`p = A(AP) \cap B(BP)` is the **orthogonal reflection** of :math:`P` across :math:`L`:

  .. math::

     p = \sigma_{AB}(P).

* Because :math:`\sigma_{AB}` is an isometry preserving distance, any geometric entity constructed on one side of :math:`AB` is immediately mirror-reflected to the other side.
* This allows Mascheroni in Libro IV to drop perpendiculars (§ 78), in Libro VII to intersect lines with circles (§ 110), and in Libro X to find circle centers (§ 143) entirely by composing reflections.

Lemoine Géométrographie Metrics
-------------------------------

Émile Lemoine (:cite:`lemoine-1902`) classified compass operations into three canonical primitives:
1. :math:`C_1`: Placing a compass divider point on a determinate point.
2. :math:`C_2`: Placing a compass divider point on an indeterminate point of a curve.
3. :math:`C_3`: Tracing a circular arc.

In Mascheroni's pure geometry, :math:`C_2` is avoided wherever possible to preserve determinacy. Each construction is characterized by its Simplicity :math:`S = m_1 + m_2 + m_3` and Exactitude :math:`E = m_1 + m_2`.
