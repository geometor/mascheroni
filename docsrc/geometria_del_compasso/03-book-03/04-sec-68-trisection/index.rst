===============================
§ 68: Exact Distance Trisection
===============================

.. folio:: 0061
   :page: 36

**68.** *Prob.* To divide the distance :math:`AB` into three equal parts.

*Sol.* To :math:`AB` add in a straight line on both sides the two distances :g:`/ E A /` (:math:`AE`), :g:`/ B V /` (:math:`BV`) equal to :math:`AB` (§ 64) (Fig. 20).

.. picture:: 0061.g1.svg
   :alt: Calibrated 5-layer vector diagram of Book III § 68 (Fig. 20): authentic compass trisection AT = Tt = tB = AB/3

.. picture:: 0061.g1.png
   :alt: Archival Fig. 20 (folio 0061, p. 36): Mascheroni's exact compass-only trisection of AB

With centers :g:`E` and :g:`V` and radius :math:`EV` describe two indefinite arcs :math:`QEq, PVp`. With the same centers :g:`E` and :g:`V` and radius :math:`EB` describe two other arcs cutting the first at :math:`Q, q, P, p`. With this same radius :math:`EB` and centers :math:`P, p` describe two arcs intersecting at :g:`t`.
With the same radius and centers

.. folio:: 0062
   :page: 37

:math:`Q, q` describe two arcs intersecting at :g:`T`. Then :math:`AB` will be divided into three parts at the two points :g:`T`, :g:`t` (:g:`/ A T /`, :g:`/ T t /`, :g:`/ t B /`).

*Dem.* The points :g:`T`, :g:`t` will lie on the straight line :math:`VE` (§ 13).
Moreover the isosceles triangle :math:`EQT` will be similar to the isosceles :math:`EQV`, having a common angle at :g:`E` (I.5 and 32; VI.4). Therefore :math:`(QE)^2 = ET \cdot EV` (VI.17). Substituting in this equation :math:`2\,AB` for :math:`QE` and :math:`3\,AB` for :math:`EV`, we get :math:`\frac{4}{3}AB = ET`; and hence :g:`/ A T /` :math:`= \frac{1}{3}AB`.
In the same manner it will be demonstrated that :g:`/ t B /` (:math:`Bt`) is also a third of :math:`AB`, and hence :g:`/ T t /` as well.
