§§ 64–65: Collinear Doubling & Integer Multiples — Summary
==========================================================

.. folio:: 0057

Collinear doubling (§ 64) and arbitrary integer multiples (§ 65): three unit-chord steps around :math:`A(AB)` construct antipodal :math:`E` in :math:`5 C_1 + 4 C_3` (:math:`S = 9`, 4 circles). Exact symbolic implementation in ``geometor.mascheroni.collinear_doubling``.
