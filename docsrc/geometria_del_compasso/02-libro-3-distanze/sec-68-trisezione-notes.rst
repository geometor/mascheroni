Harmonic Inversion Mechanics
----------------------------

Mascheroni's trisection solution illustrates the underlying principle of circular inversion that unifies *La Geometria del Compasso*:
* The point :math:`M` is the inverse of :math:`E` with respect to the circle centered at :math:`B` with radius :math:`R = AB`:

  .. math::

     BM \cdot BE = R^2 \implies BM = \frac{R^2}{BE}.

* When :math:`BE = 2 R`, :math:`BM = \frac{1}{2} R` (bisection, § 66).
* When :math:`BE = 3 R`, :math:`BM = \frac{1}{3} R` (trisection, § 68).
* In general, by extending :math:`BE = n R` via collinear repetition (§ 64), any harmonic fraction :math:`\frac{1}{n} AB` is constructed with compass alone.
