=========================================
§§ 36–39: Chord Lemmas & 48-Part Division
=========================================

.. folio:: 0051
   :page: 22

**§§ 36–37 (Fig. 10).** Mascheroni proves the exact radical half-chord relations :math:`\sin 67^\circ 30' = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}}` and :math:`\sin 22^\circ 30' = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2 - \sqrt{2}}`, showing how nested square roots arise directly from repeated compass intersections on a single circle.

**§§ 38–39 (Fig. 11).** Exact division of the circumference into **48 equal parts** (:math:`7^\circ 30'`) by bisecting the :math:`15^\circ` octant-duodecimal arcs purely with compass openings.
