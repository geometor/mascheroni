===========================================================
§§ 146–149: Inscribing & Circumscribing Circles to Polygons
===========================================================

.. folio:: 0108
   :page: 83

**§§ 146–149 (Figs. 72–74, folio 0108–folio 0110, pp. 83–85).** Applying the semicircle third-proportional method of § 143 directly to three consecutive vertices, Mascheroni finds the common center :math:`O`, circumradius, and inradius (by midpoint bisection of one side, § 66) for an equilateral triangle (§ 146, Fig. 72), a square (§ 147, Fig. 73), and any regular :math:`n`-gon (§§ 148–149, Fig. 74).
