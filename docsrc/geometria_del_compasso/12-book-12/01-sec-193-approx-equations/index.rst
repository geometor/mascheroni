=============================================================
§§ 193–214: Fundamental Chord Equations & Sexagesimal Degrees
=============================================================

.. folio:: 0145
   :page: 120

**§§ 193–214 (folio 0145–folio 0151, pp. 120–126).** Because angles such as :math:`1^\circ` or :math:`1'` cannot be constructed algebraically by straightedge and compass (angle trisection is cubic), Mascheroni turns the master points of Book II Fig. 12 (:math:`a, b, e, \dots`) into an **ultra-high-precision compass engine**.

Deriving Equation (1) (:math:`\cos A' = 2\sin A \cos 45^\circ - \frac{1}{2}`, §§ 201–203, folio 0148), Mascheroni proves that stepping :math:`aZ` from :math:`BZ = 18^\circ` determines :math:`A' = 93^\circ 36' 40''` to **better than one third-second** (:math:`< 1''' = \frac{1}{60}''`, error :math:`< 10^{-7}` radians!), enabling exact-to-the-eye single-degree circle graduation.
