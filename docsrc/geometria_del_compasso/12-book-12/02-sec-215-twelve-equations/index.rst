=======================================================
§§ 215–235: The Twelve Logarithmic Graduation Equations
=======================================================

.. folio:: 0152
   :page: 127

**§§ 215–235 (folio 0152–folio 0158, pp. 127–133).** Mascheroni systematically pairs the exact radical points of Fig. 12 (:math:`a, b, e, \dots`, involving :math:`\sqrt{2}`, :math:`\sqrt{5}`, and :math:`\sqrt{2 \pm \sqrt{2}}`) to assemble **twelve factored trigonometric equations** formatted specifically for logarithmic table computation (e.g., Equation [10] on folio 0155: :math:`\sin E = 4\sin\frac{A-30^\circ}{2}\cos\frac{A+30^\circ}{2}\sin 67^\circ 30'`). Each single compass transfer maps a known constructible arc into a dense web of precision targets.
