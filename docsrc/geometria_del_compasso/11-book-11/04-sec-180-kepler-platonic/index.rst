========================================================
§§ 180–185: Kepler Triangle & Platonic Solid Circumradii
========================================================

.. folio:: 0131
   :page: 106

**§ 180 (folio 0131, p. 106).** Compass construction of the 3–4–5 right triangle whose sides stand in arithmetic progression (:math:`\frac{6}{5}, \frac{8}{5}, \frac{10}{5}`).

**§ 181. Problem (Fig. 91, folio 0132, p. 107).** Construct a right triangle :math:`BNE` whose three sides stand in **geometric progression** (the celebrated **Kepler triangle**!).

*Construction:* On unit circle :g:`( A B )` with diameter :g:`/ B E /` :math:`= 2`, locate the Golden Section point :g:`beta` (:math:`A\beta = \frac{\sqrt{5} - 1}{2}`) and its symmetric twin :g:`b` (:g:`/ beta b /` :math:`= \sqrt{5} - 1 = 2\phi^{-1}`) via Book II § 46. Sweep arc :g:`( E N )` of radius :math:`b\beta` from :g:`E` cutting the circle at :g:`N`. Then :g:`/ N E /` :math:`= \sqrt{5} - 1`, :g:`/ B N /` :math:`= \sqrt{2(\sqrt{5} - 1)}`, and :g:`/ B E /` :math:`= 2`, satisfying :math:`BE : BN :: BN : NE = \sqrt{\phi}`!

**§§ 182–185 (Fig. 93, folio 0132–folio 0137, pp. 107–112).** Mascheroni's *Lemma 182* tabulates the exact circumsphere radii of all **five Platonic solids** of unit edge :math:`1` and constructs each one directly with the compass:

* **Tetrahedron:** :math:`\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\frac{3}{2}}`
* **Cube:** :math:`\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{3}`
* **Octahedron:** :math:`\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}`
* **Dodecahedron:** :math:`\frac{1}{4}\sqrt{3}\,(\sqrt{5} + 1) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\phi`
* **Icosahedron:** :math:`\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\frac{5 + \sqrt{5}}{2}}`
