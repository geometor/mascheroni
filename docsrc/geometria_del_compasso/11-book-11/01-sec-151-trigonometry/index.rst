======================================================
§§ 151–160: Compass Proofs of Trigonometric Identities
======================================================

.. folio:: 0111
   :page: 86

**§§ 151–160 (Figs. 76–78, folio 0111–folio 0114, pp. 86–89).** Opening Book XI, Mascheroni develops self-contained compass-geometry derivations of the addition, product-to-sum, half-angle, and chord identities (:math:`\text{chord } x = \sqrt{2 - 2\cos x}`, :math:`k^2 = 2 - 2c`, :math:`h^2 = 2 - 2k\sqrt{1 - \frac{1}{4}k^2}`, § 160), equipping the reader for the precision arc calculations of Book XII.
