Commentary & Notes: Libro I — The Epistemology of Instrument Redundancy
=======================================================================

.. rubric:: The Foundational Shift from Continuous Lines to Point-Pairs

1. Why Euclid Postulated the Straightedge
-----------------------------------------

In classical antiquity, :person:`Euclid <euclid>` grounded geometry in the physical mechanics of the drafting table: drawing an actual graphite or ink line along a straightedge creates an immediate visual representation of continuous linear space.

However, as :person:`Lorenzo Mascheroni <mascheroni>` recognized, the **straight line in synthetic proofs functions almost exclusively as an incidence carrier between points**:

* We draw a line :math:`AB` to determine its intersection with another line :math:`CD`, or to project a perpendicular from :math:`P`.
* The physical continuum of points along the line segment between :math:`A` and :math:`B` is rarely used; only specific landmark points (midpoints, harmonic conjugates, section cuts) enter the chain of demonstration.
* By demonstrating that all such target points can be located by circular intersections alone, Mascheroni revealed that **continuous straight lines are logically redundant for constructive plane geometry**.

2. Physical Precision: The Advantage of the Fixed Compass
---------------------------------------------------------

Draftsmen and instrument makers in eighteenth-century Pavia and Milan had long known that straightedges introduce unavoidable mechanical errors:

* Any ruler edge has microscopic warp, bevel irregularities, and parallex errors when guiding a drawing pen.
* A well-machined steel compass with a locking screw maintains a fixed radius with exceptional mechanical stability.
* In Lemoine's exactitude metric :math:`E = m_1 + m_2`, compass arcs avoid the double alignment :math:`2R_1` required each time a straightedge must be brought into simultaneous contact with two points on paper.
