Commentary & Notes: Libro IV — The Parallelogram and Thales Invariance
======================================================================

.. rubric:: Orthogonal Reflection as a Universal Metric Operator

1. The Dual Metric Roles of the Compass
---------------------------------------

Book IV demonstrates how the compass simultaneously executes two distinct synthetic roles:

1. **Length preservation (Isometry)**: Transporting rigid distances :math:`AC` and :math:`AB` to opposite vertices ensures equal opposite sides, producing an affine translation without invoking continuous angular measurement.
2. **Orthogonal symmetry (Reflection)**: Intersecting two circles centered along line :math:`AB` automatically generates a line of symmetry :math:`DE \perp AB`. The reflection partner :math:`E` requires zero linear guidance; it is an intrinsic consequence of the circular metric.

2. Fazzari's Pedagogical Reflection
-----------------------------------

In his notes to § 80, :person:`Gaetano Fazzari <fazzari>` (:cite:`fazzari-1901`) noted that secondary school curricula that mandate straightedges often confuse students with transversal-angle theorems (alternate interior angles, corresponding angles), whereas Mascheroni's pure two-arc parallel is self-evident from the definition of a parallelogram.
