===========================
To Bonaparte the Italique
===========================

.. rubric:: Lorenzo Mascheroni (*The Geometry of the Compass*, Pavia 1797, p. 1)

.. folio:: 0030

.. line-block::

   I too beheld you with unconquered hand,
     Which divides kingdoms and to Vienna dictated peace,
     Share with me in attentive gaze
     The curved sweep of the faithful compass.
   And you I saw decipher the secret ciphers
     Of arduous problems with the prowess of an ancient
     Master Geometer; and I recalled
     When you crossed the Alps, a new Hannibal,
     To deliver your beloved Italy, and all
     Passed swiftly before my eyes:
     The year of your victories, a year that shines
     In the abyss of the centuries like a sun.
   Pursue your enterprise, and with unconquered hand
     Lead your Italy into days of freedom.
