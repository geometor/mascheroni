Bibliography¶

Primary Archival Witnesses, Translations & Critical Restricted-Instrument Literature

Primary Archival Editions & Witnesses¶

[mascheroni-1797] Lorenzo Mascheroni (1797)¶

  • Author: Lorenzo Mascheroni

  • Title: La Geometria del Compasso

  • Locus: Libri I–XII, §§ 1–280, pp. 1–151, 14 folding plates (1797) / 108 inline figures (1901)

  • Year: 1797 (Anno V della Repubblica Francese)

  • Publisher: Presso gli Eredi di Pietro Galeazzi, Pavia

  • Archival Witness: 300-DPI archival scan in resources/geometria_del_compasso/pdfs/mascheroni_1797_geometria_del_compasso.pdf and visual transcription in resources/geometria_del_compasso/sources/mascheroni_1797_compasso_it.txt.

  • Note: Classic compass-only treatise proving constructively across twelve books that every Euclidean point determination can be executed with the compass alone.

[carette-1798] Antoine-Michel Carette (trans., 1798)¶

  • Author: Lorenzo Mascheroni (trans. Antoine-Michel Carette)

  • Title: Géométrie du compas, par L. Mascheroni

  • Locus: xvi + 263 pp., 14 folding plates

  • Year: 1798 (An VI)

  • Publisher: Duprat, Libraire pour les Mathématiques, Paris

  • Note: Authoritative French translation through which Lemoine, Poncelet, and European geometers cited Mascheroni’s problem numbering and compass-only proofs.

[fazzari-1901] Gaetano Fazzari (ed., 1901)¶

  • Author: Lorenzo Mascheroni (ed. Gaetano Fazzari)

  • Title: La Geometria del Compasso: Nuova edizione con prefazione e note

  • Locus: Prefazione storica pp. v–xvi; Testo pp. 1–152

  • Year: 1901

  • Publisher: Casa Editrice ‘Era Nova’, Palermo

  • Note: Centenary critical Italian edition whose historical preface (p. xi) explicitly evaluates Mascheroni’s constructions through Lemoine’s Géométrographie.

Historical Predecessors & Dual Restricted-Instrument Canons¶

[mohr-1672] Georg Mohr (1672)¶

  • Author: Georg Mohr

  • Title: Euclides Danicus, Bestaende udi Too Deele

  • Locus: Parts I–II, 36 pp. (republished with German translation by J. Hjelmslev, Copenhagen: Høst & Søn, 1928)

  • Year: 1672

  • Publisher: Jacob van Velsen, Amsterdam

  • Note: Earliest complete proof of compass-only Euclidean constructibility, anticipating Mascheroni by 125 years.

[adler-1890] August Adler (1890)¶

  • Author: August Adler

  • Title: Zur Theorie der Mascheronischen Constructionen

  • Locus: Sitzungsberichte der Kaiserlichen Akademie der Wissenschaften in Wien, Math.-Naturwiss. Classe, Bd. 99, Abt. IIa, pp. 910–916; Theorie der geometrischen Konstruktionen, Sammlung Göschen Nr. 301 (Leipzig, 1906)

  • Year: 1890 / 1906

  • Publisher: K.-K. Hof- und Staatsdruckerei, Vienna / G. J. Göschen, Leipzig

  • Note: Modern conceptual proof of the Mohr–Mascheroni theorem via circular inversion (\(5C_1 + 3C_3\)), reducing line–line and line–circle meets directly to circle–circle intersections.

[lemoine-1902] Émile Lemoine (1902)¶

  • Author: Émile Lemoine

  • Title: Géométrographie, ou Art des constructions géométriques

  • Locus: Collection Scientia, Série Physico-Mathématique, n° 18, 88 pp.

  • Year: 1902

  • Publisher: C. Naud, Éditeur, Paris

  • Note: Monograph formalizing Géométrographie Mascheronienne (§ 2, pp. 66–74) with exact operational symbols \(m_1 C_1 + m_2 C_2 + m_3 C_3\), simplicity \(S\), and exactitude \(E\).

[steiner-1833] Jakob Steiner (1833)¶

  • Author: Jakob Steiner

  • Title: Die geometrischen Constructionen, ausgeführt mittelst der geraden Linie und Eines festen Kreises

  • Locus: §§ 1–22, [1]–[110]; Ostwald’s Klassiker der exakten Wissenschaften Nr. 60 (1895)

  • Year: 1833

  • Publisher: Ferdinand Dümmler, Berlin

  • Note: Demonstrates the dual minimal-instrument regime: straightedge alone with one fixed circle and its center solves all Euclidean problems.

[euclid-heath] Euclid (trans. Heath, 1908/1926)¶

  • Author: Euclid (trans. Sir Thomas L. Heath)

  • Title: The Thirteen Books of Euclid’s Elements

  • Locus: 3 vols. (Books I–XIII); GEOMETOR Digital Scriptorium Edition

  • Year: 1908 / 1926

  • Publisher: Cambridge University Press

  • Note: Definitive English philological edition providing the axiomatic propositions benchmarked against compass-only constructions.

[hilbert-1899] David Hilbert (1899)¶

  • Author: David Hilbert

  • Title: Grundlagen der Geometrie

  • Locus: Siebentes Kapitel: Die geometrischen Konstruktionen auf Grund der Axiome I–IV (§§ 36–39)

  • Year: 1899

  • Publisher: B. G. Teubner, Leipzig

  • Note: Algebraic criteria (pythagorean vs. Euclidean quadratic closure) characterizing constructibility under restricted instruments.
