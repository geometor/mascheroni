Lorenzo Mascheroni¶

The works of Lorenzo Mascheroni (1750–1800) proved that every Euclidean construction can be executed with the compass alone, establishing the foundations of compass-only geometry in his 1797 masterpiece La Geometria del Compasso. Within the interconnected GEOMETOR semantic digital archive, these foundational texts are liberated from static print bounds into addressable semantic units, calibrated vector diagrams, primary-source folio provenance, and exact symbolic models speaking one shared geometric language.
