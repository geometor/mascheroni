Summary: Notation & Fundamental Collinearity Lemma¶

Structural Decomposition of Orthogonal Reflection

Problem Formulation: The Fundamental Lemma (§ 13)¶

Given:

Two fixed points \(A, B\) defining baseline \(AB\). Two radii \(r_1 = AP\) and \(r_2 = AQ\).

Construct:

  1. Intersect \(A(r_1) \cap B(BP)\) to determine point pair \((P, p)\).

  2. Intersect \(A(r_2) \cap B(BQ)\) to determine point pair \((Q, q)\).

Demonstrate:

  1. Triangles \(\triangle APB \cong \triangle ApB\) by SSS. Hence \(\angle PAB = \angle pAB\) and \(\angle PBA = \angle pBA\).

  2. Segment \(Pp\) is the orthogonal reflection axis: the line of centers \(AB\) is the perpendicular bisector of \(Pp\).

  3. The points \(Q, P, p, q\) all lie on the perpendicular bisector of \(AB\).

  4. Hence, collinearity of four points is established purely through concentric circular symmetry without drawing any lines.

Operational Complexity (Lemoine OpCount):

  • Intersection of two circles with centers \(A, B\): \(3C_1 + C_3\) (first circle) \(+ 2C_1 + C_3\) (second circle) = \(5C_1 + 2C_3\).

  • Simplicity \(S = 7\), Exactitude \(E = 5\), Circles \(= 2\).
