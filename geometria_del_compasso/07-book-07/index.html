Book VII: Intersections of Lines & Circles¶

Libro Settimo: Della intersezione delle rette cogli archi di cerchio e tra loro (§§ 110–112)

The theoretical climax of La Geometria del Compasso (§§ 110–112, pp. 60–63, folios 0085–0088): locating the intersection of a virtual line with a circle (§§ 110–111) and the intersection of two virtual lines (§ 112) by compass alone—completing the constructive proof of the Mohr–Mascheroni theorem.

§ 111: Line–Circle Intersection Through the Center

111. Problem. Given an arc \(BCD\) described with center \(A\) (fig. 48); to find the two points where the straight line passing through \(A\) and another given point \(L\) cuts the circumference.

§ 112: Intersection of Two Virtual Lines & Circular Inversion

112. Problem. Given two points \(A, B\) of one straight line (figs. 49 and 50), and two points \(C, D\) of another; to find the point \(S\) where they intersect.
