Summary: Fazzari’s 1901 Historical Preface¶

The Centenary Revival and Lemoine’s Operational Metric

Historiographical Purpose¶

Gaetano Fazzari’s 1901 Palermo centenary edition marked the modern critical rehabilitation of Lorenzo Mascheroni in Italian mathematical scholarship. Published by Casa Editrice “Era Nova”, the edition made Mascheroni’s rare 1797 Pavia treatise widely accessible to twentieth-century geometers.

Key Historiographical Themes¶

  1. Euclidean Style vs. Operational Modernity: Fazzari explicitly distinguishes between Mascheroni’s classical Euclidean demonstration style (synthetically chained back to Euclid’s Elements) and his modern constructive power. While the proofs can be streamlined by modern vector algebra or complex numbers, the operational sequences remain optimal.

  2. First Application of Géométrographie: Fazzari is the first editor to explicitly recommend applying Émile Lemoine’s Géométrographie (presented at the French Association for the Advancement of Science in 1888) to Mascheroni’s problems, validating their Simplicity ($S$) and Exactitude ($E$).

  3. Organic Transformation Theory: Mascheroni did not merely collect isolated problems; he developed a systematic algorithm for translating any step $(L_1, L_2)$ of a straightedge construction into circular operations $(C_1, C_2, C_3)$.
