Notes: Fazzari’s 1901 Historical Preface¶

Editorial and Bibliographical Apparatus

Gaetano Fazzari and Il Pitagora¶

Gaetano Fazzari (1856–1935) was a professor of mathematics at the Liceo Umberto I in Palermo and lecturer in the history of mathematics at the University of Palermo. In 1895, he founded Il Pitagora: Giornale di Matematica per gli studenti delle scuole medie, a journal instrumental in fostering geometric inquiry and historical scholarship among Italian students and teachers.

The Priority Dispute and Georg Mohr¶

When Fazzari published his centenary edition in 1901, the mathematical world universally regarded Lorenzo Mascheroni as the sole discoverer of the compass-only theorem. It was not until 1928 that the Danish mathematician Johannes Hjelmslev discovered a copy of Georg Mohr’s Dutch treatise Euclides Danicus (Amsterdam, 1672) in a Copenhagen bookstore, proving that Mohr had independently anticipated the result 125 years prior.

However, Mohr’s work had vanished completely from European awareness and exerted zero influence on subsequent mathematics, whereas Mascheroni’s 1797 work directly influenced Napoleon, Lagrange, Laplace, Poncelet, Steiner, and Adler, establishing the field of constructive geometry.
