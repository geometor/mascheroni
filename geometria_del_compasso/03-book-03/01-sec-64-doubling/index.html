§§ 64–65: Collinear Doubling & Integer Multiples¶

§ 64. Problem. Given two points B and A, find a third point E on the virtual line \((B, A)\) such that (hence \(BE = 2\,AB\)).

Construction: With center A and radius BA, describe circle AB. Using the same compass opening BA, step three successive chords around the circle from B: BC locating C, CD locating D, and DE locating E (). Because three inscribed equilateral triangles span \(3 \times 60^\circ = 180^\circ\), B, A, E are strictly collinear and \(BE = 2\,AB\).

§ 65. Repeating collinear doubling iteratively generates any integer multiple \(n \cdot AB\) along the virtual line without ever using a straightedge.
