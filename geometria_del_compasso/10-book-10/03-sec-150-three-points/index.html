§ 150: Circumcenter Through Three Points & Steiner Duality¶

150. Problem. To find the center \(S\) of a circle (fig. 75) passing through three given points \(PQR\).

Solution. Taking centers at \(P\) and \(Q\), with an arbitrary radius mark two arcs intersecting at \(A\) and \(B\). Taking centers at \(Q\) and \(R\), mark likewise with an arbitrary radius two arcs intersecting at \(C\) and \(D\). Find the point \(S\) where the two lines \(AB\), \(CD\) intersect (§ 112). \(S\) will be the center sought.

Demonstration. See Prop. 25, Book III.
