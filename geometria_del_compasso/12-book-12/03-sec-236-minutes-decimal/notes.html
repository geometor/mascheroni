Witness & Folio Provenance¶

  • Folio Span: 0159–0167 (printed pp. 134–142)

Folios 0159–0167 correspond to printed pages 134–142 (fazzari-1901). Historically links La Geometria del Compasso directly to Mascheroni’s 1798–1800 Paris appointment to the International Commission on Weights and Measures.
