2. Exact Ratio Conservation¶

By using concentric circles centered at an arbitrary origin \(O\), Mascheroni creates an angle-preserving dilation (homothety) centered at \(O\):

\[h_{O, b/a} : \Gamma_a \to \Gamma_b, \qquad \overline{DE} = \frac{b}{a} \overline{BC}.\]

Because rotation about \(O\) is an isometry, transferring chord \(BC\) onto \(\Gamma_b\) requires only compass radius setting, eliminating the entire machinery of parallel transversals!
