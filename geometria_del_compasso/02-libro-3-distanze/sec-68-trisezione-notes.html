Notes: Distance Trisection in a Straight Line¶

Rational Segment Division and Inversion Geometry

Harmonic Inversion Mechanics¶

Mascheroni’s trisection solution illustrates the underlying principle of circular inversion that unifies La Geometria del Compasso: * The point \(M\) is the inverse of \(E\) with respect to the circle centered at \(B\) with radius \(R = AB\):

\[BM \cdot BE = R^2 \implies BM = \frac{R^2}{BE}.\]

  • When \(BE = 2 R\), \(BM = \frac{1}{2} R\) (bisection, § 66).

  • When \(BE = 3 R\), \(BM = \frac{1}{3} R\) (trisection, § 68).

  • In general, by extending \(BE = n R\) via collinear repetition (§ 64), any harmonic fraction \(\frac{1}{n} AB\) is constructed with compass alone.
