Summary: Segment Bisection (Solution V)¶

Structured Decomposition and Operational Complexity

Problem Formulation (§ 66, Sol. V)¶

Given:

Two distinct points \(A, B\) defining baseline segment \(AB\).

Construct:

  1. Collinear doubling: With center \(A\) and radius \(AB\), construct collinear point \(E\) such that \(AE = AB\) and \(E, A, B\) are collinear (\(BE = 2 AB\), § 64).

  2. Large arc: With center \(E\) and radius \(EB = 2 AB\), describe arc \(\Gamma = E(2 AB)\).

  3. Circle at \(B\): With center \(B\) and radius \(BA = AB\), cut arc \(\Gamma\) at points \(P\) and \(p\).

  4. Final intersection: With centers \(P\) and \(p\) and radius \(BA = AB\), describe arcs intersecting at \(M\).

  5. The point \(M\) is the exact midpoint of \(AB\).

Demonstrate:

  • Triangle \(\triangle PBE\) is isosceles with \(EP = EB = 2 AB\) and \(BP = AB\).

  • Triangle \(\triangle PBM\) is isosceles with \(BP = MP = AB\) and base \(PM\).

  • They share the base angle \(\angle PBE = \angle PBM\).

  • By similarity of isosceles triangles sharing a base angle:

    \[\frac{BE}{BP} = \frac{BP}{BM} \implies BM = \frac{BP^2}{BE} = \frac{AB^2}{2 AB} = \frac{1}{2} AB.\]
Operational Complexity (Lemoine OpCount):

  • Formula: \(10C_1 + 7C_3\)

  • Simplicity: \(S = 17\)

  • Exactitude: \(E = 10\)

  • Total circles: \(5\)

  • Distinct radii: \(2\) (\(R_1 = AB\), \(R_2 = 2 AB\)).
