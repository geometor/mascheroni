Archival Table of Contents (Indice)¶

The closing table of contents (Indice, folio 0177) of Gaetano Fazzari’s 1901 Palermo edition lists the complete canonical structure of the treatise as preserved across this Digital Scriptorium codex:

  • Prefazione storica (Fazzari) — pp. III–XVI (folio 0016–folio 0029)

  • A Bonaparte l’italico — p. 1 (folio 0030)

  • Prefazione (Mascheroni) — pp. 3–9 (folio 0032–folio 0038)

  • Book I (Preliminare, §§ 1–26) — p. 11 (folio 0040)

  • Book II (Division of the circumference and circular arcs, §§ 27–63) — p. 19 (folio 0048)

  • Book III (Multiplication and division of straight distances, §§ 64–70) — p. 31 (folio 0057)

  • Book IV (Addition and subtraction; perpendiculars and parallels, §§ 71–85) — p. 40 (folio 0065)

  • Book V (Proportional distances, §§ 86–99) — p. 46 (folio 0071)

  • Book VI (Square roots, §§ 100–109) — p. 51 (folio 0076)

  • Book VII (Intersections of straight lines with circular arcs and with one another, §§ 110–112) — p. 60 (folio 0085)

  • Book VIII (Angles and trigonometric lines, §§ 113–123) — p. 63 (folio 0088)

  • Book IX (Similar figures and regular polygons, §§ 124–142) — p. 68 (folio 0093)

  • Book X (Centers, §§ 143–150) — p. 82 (folio 0107)

  • Book XI (Miscellaneous problems, §§ 151–192) — p. 86 (folio 0111)

  • Book XII (Problems by approximation, §§ 193–280) — p. 120 (folio 0145–folio 0176)
