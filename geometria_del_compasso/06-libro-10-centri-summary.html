Center Determination: Mascheroni vs. Steiner¶

2. Algebraic Invariants of Napoleon’s Problem¶

For given circle \(\Gamma(O, R)\) and arbitrary point \(A\) on \(\Gamma\):

\[AB = AC = r, \qquad AD = \frac{r^2}{R}, \qquad \text{Inversion of } D \text{ through } A(r) \implies AO = \frac{r^2}{AD} = R.\]

Because \(D\) lies on diameter \(AO\), the inverse of \(D\) through circle \(A(r)\) falls at distance \(R\) from \(A\) along the diameter, which is precisely the center \(O\)!
